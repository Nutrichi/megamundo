Rockstar is releasing GTA Online: A Safehouse in the Hills in December. The
update adds mansion properties from Prix Luxury Real Estate in exclusive Los
Santos neighbourhoods. Rockstar gives no release date and no prices in the
announcement.

The estates have sprawling grounds and first-rate amenities, according to
Rockstar. The announcement mentions personal assistance and business service
technology, but does not explain what those do.

## What players can do now

Becoming VIP through 7 December earns a reward package. It includes a
discount on the mansion properties once they arrive and the Black Rockstar
Varsity Crewneck.

A sports car in the Sessanta Nove Monogram livery can be claimed at the
Luxury Autos showroom. Rockstar does not name the model in plain text.

## This week

The Diamond Casino and Resort has a bonus running this week. The finale of
The Diamond Casino Heist pays double rewards.

Which mansions will be available in December, and what they will cost, is
not stated.
