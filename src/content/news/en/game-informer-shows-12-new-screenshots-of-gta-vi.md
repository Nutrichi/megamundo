Game Informer has published 12 new screenshots of Grand Theft Auto VI. They
are part of a cover story of 14 pages, for which the magazine spoke with
Rockstar Games. The digital edition appeared on 29 September.

Besides the images, the story covers the weather, the animals of Leonida and
the research Rockstar did in Florida. Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos appear in
several of the screenshots.

## The screenshots
