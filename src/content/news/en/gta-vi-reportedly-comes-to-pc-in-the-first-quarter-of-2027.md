Grand Theft Auto VI comes to PC in the first quarter of 2027, GamesBeat
reports. That is a few months after the release on PlayStation 5 and Xbox
Series X and S. The site cites four anonymous sources. Rockstar Games has not
commented.

## Four sources, no confirmation

GamesBeat spoke to four industry sources who know the plans of the studio.
All four asked to stay anonymous to protect their business relationships.
Rockstar did not respond to a request for comment, the site writes. Take-Two
has not commented either, IGN notes.

A version for PC has never been announced. When Rockstar set the date of
[19 November 2026](/news/rockstar-delays-gta-vi-to-19-november-2026/), it
named only PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S.

## Much faster than GTA V

Grand Theft Auto V came out on consoles in September 2013. The PC version
followed in April 2015, about a year and seven months later. A gap of a few
months would be much shorter, GamesBeat notes.

Red Dead Redemption, L.A. Noire, Max Payne 3 and Red Dead Redemption 2 also
reached PC only after the consoles, IGN points out. GTA V still has a large
group of players on PC, through GTA Online and roleplay servers.

## The link with online play

According to GamesBeat, the PC version matters for the online side of the
game. Rockstar bought Cfx.re, the team behind the roleplay framework FiveM,
in August 2023. FiveM only runs on the PC version of GTA V.

Rockstar has not announced an online mode for GTA VI. Twitch chief executive
Dan Clancy told Bloomberg at Tokyo Game Show that online play for GTA VI
arrives next year. Fans call it GTA Online 2 for now.

Filings in a tribunal in September already mentioned an
[online format for 32 players](/news/rockstar-calls-a-32-player-online-format-for-gta-vi-a-guarded-secret/).
Rockstar called that format a closely guarded secret.

## What Take-Two said earlier

Strauss Zelnick, the head of Take-Two, has always referred questions about a
PC version to Rockstar. In May, Zelnick told Bloomberg that Rockstar always
starts on console, to serve its core audience first.

In November 2025, Zelnick said on CNBC that the video game business is moving
towards PC. The industry is trending towards open rather than closed
platforms, according to Zelnick.

## What is not known

There is no release date and no price for the PC version. The system
requirements are not known either. Rockstar has not shared the frame rate or
resolution of the console version. Whether the PC version and online play
arrive together is also not known.
