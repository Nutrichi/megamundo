GTA Online is getting a new heist. In The Kortz Center Heist, players raid
an art gallery in the hills of Pacific Bluffs. An Art Studio has to be added
to a Mansion property first. Rockstar names July, and the announcement page
gives 14 July.

The heist runs in several stages. Players scope out the complex, choose an
approach, and then have to get in and back out again.

## Weeks of build-up

Rockstar says the weeks before the update will carry bonus GTA$ rewards and
discounts. A discount of GTA$2,000,000 on a Mansion property is named.

The update comes to PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Xbox Series X and S, Xbox
One, and PC.

## A free upgrade for GTAV

In the same announcement Rockstar opens a free upgrade path for Grand Theft
Auto V. Owners of any PlayStation 4 version, or of the digital Xbox One
version, can move to the PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X and S version at no
cost. On PC, players can move from Legacy to Enhanced for free.

## What is still unknown

How many stages the heist has is not stated. What it pays out is not
mentioned either, and neither is how many players are needed.
