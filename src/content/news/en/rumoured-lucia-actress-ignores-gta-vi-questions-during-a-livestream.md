Manni L. Perez, the actress fans have linked to Lucia for years, went live on
Instagram and left every question about Grand Theft Auto VI unanswered.
Recordings of the stream went viral, IGN writes. Rockstar Games has not
confirmed the casting.

The stream lasted 12 minutes and peaked at more than 2,100 viewers, according
to IGN. The account is six months old and holds nine posts. Perez talked about
growing up in a small rural town, about cooking and about wanting a green
truck.

The chat filled up with comments about Lucia and GTA VI. Some viewers asked
whether Perez would play the game, others whether Perez was ready for next
month. Only comments about other subjects got an answer, IGN writes.

A user posted a recording of the stream on Reddit.

## Fans point to earlier roles

The link to Lucia rests on clues that fans have gathered over several years.
Perez has a history of live action and voice work. IGN lists single episodes
of Jessica Jones, The Blacklist and Law & Order: Special Victims Unit. Perez
also played a blackjack dealer in Grand Theft Auto Online.

Perez has denied the rumours about the role before. Even if the role were
real, a non disclosure agreement would cover it. Actors were
[reportedly reminded](/news/gta-vi-actors-were-reportedly-reminded-their-nda-runs-until-19-november/)
that theirs runs until 19 November, the day GTA VI comes out.

## The rest of the cast

Rockstar has never published a cast list. Fans have long named Dylan Rourke as
the actor behind Jason, but Rourke has not been confirmed either. Stephen Root
is so far the only actor who confirmed being in the game. Fans link Root to the
character Brian Heder.

Rockstar describes [Lucia Caminos](/game/characters/lucia-caminos/) on its
site but names no actress. GTA VI comes out on 19 November for PlayStation 5
and Xbox Series X|S.
