GTA+ members can claim the Pegassi Horus from 10 September. The supercar is
free at The Vinewood Car Club. It stays exclusive to members for one week,
after which it goes on sale to everyone at Legendary Motorsport.

The car comes wrapped in a livery that Rockstar shows in the announcement.
Rockstar describes the Horus as a sleek supercar and gives no performance
figures.

## The rest of the month for members

Cyan/Red Flip Chameleon Paint and the matching wheel paint are free at all
vehicle workshops and mod bays. The Bigness Patchwork Revere Collar is added
to member wardrobes automatically.

The first Cluckin' Bell Farm Raid finale each week pays double GTA$. That
runs from 10 September to 7 October, says Rockstar. Cocaine production speeds
are doubled for member biker businesses this month.

Members also receive GTA$500,000 in their Maze Bank account on billing, and
can buy member-only Shark Cards with 15 percent bonus cash.

## What is still unknown

Rockstar does not give a price for the Horus once it reaches Legendary
Motorsport. The exact date it becomes available to everyone is not in the
announcement either. Top speed, handling and other figures are not
mentioned.
