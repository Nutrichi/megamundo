Rockstar has made more vehicles in GTA Online eligible for Drift Tuning. The
studio is also adding a new import, the Shitzu Keitora. Drift Races pay
triple during the Drift Event. The Drift Tuning upgrades themselves are
discounted.

The Keitora is a small vehicle in the Utility class and is sold at Southern
San Andreas Super Autos. It can be tested for free on the test track inside
the LS Car Meet.

## What the event pays out

All Drift Races pay triple this week. Completing three of them satisfies the
weekly challenge. The Walton L35 returns as a free vehicle.

Players who are not yet a member of the LS Car Meet can sign up with Mimi at
the club in East Los Santos. Contracts from the job board in an Auto Shop
pay a bonus this week.

Showrooms across Los Santos are filled with Japanese imports during the
event, many of them carrying sponsor decals and race liveries.

## What is still unknown

Whether the Drift Tuning upgrades stay discounted after this week is not
stated. What the Keitora costs is not mentioned in the announcement
either.
