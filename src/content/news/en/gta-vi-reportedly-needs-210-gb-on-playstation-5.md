Grand Theft Auto VI reportedly takes up 210 GB on the PlayStation 5. That
figure comes from the X account PlayStation Game Size, Tweakers writes. It
applies to the standard edition. Rockstar Games has not yet announced how large
the game is.

PlayStation Game Size posted the figure on 27 October, together with the dates
of the preload and the release.

## The Ultimate Edition is not known

How large the Ultimate Edition is, is not known. That edition adds exclusive
vehicles and weapons, among other things. It costs 99,99 euro, 20 euro more
than the standard edition.

## Larger than Red Dead Redemption 2

At 210 GB, GTA VI would be the largest game from Rockstar so far, Tweakers
writes. The digital version of Red Dead Redemption 2 takes up 149 GB.

The [preload starts on 12 November](/news/gta-vi-preload-starts-on-12-november-and-needs-free-space/),
a week before the release. Grand Theft Auto VI comes out on 19 November for
PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S.
