Boobie Ike is a local legend in Vice City, and according to Rockstar he acts
like one. He is one of the few who turned a past on the streets into a
legitimate business.

That business covers real estate, a strip club and a recording studio.
Rockstar describes him as all smiles, until the conversation turns to
business.

What he is most invested in is Only Raw Records, the label he runs with the
young Dre'Quan Priest. According to Rockstar, the label still needs a hit.
