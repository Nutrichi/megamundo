Rockstar has started the Neighborhood Watch event in GTA Online. Two new
Bravado Buffalo law enforcement vehicles come with it. GTA+ members can
claim the Buffalo STX Pursuit free, a week before other players get it on
9 April.

The second vehicle is sold through Warstock Cache & Carry and is on display
at The Vinewood Car Club. Members get 20% off that one.

## A mission is required first

Buying a decommissioned law enforcement vehicle is only possible after
finishing The Cluckin' Bell Farm Raid, Rockstar says. Seven older police
vehicles are 35% off at Warstock through 15 April.

Dispatch Work has to be unlocked separately. That happens by completing the
Slush Fund mission from the same raid as leader.

## Missions and outfits

New Dispatch Work missions pay double for two weeks. They include tracking a
fugitive with a scanner aboard a Buzzard and defusing a bomb on a prison
transport.

Two weekly challenges award police uniforms. Five Dispatch Work missions
between 2 and 8 April give a summer LSPD outfit. Two Bail Office bounties
between 9 and 15 April give a winter one.

Owners of the Declasse Park Ranger who log in during the first week receive
ranger outfits. That vehicle is only sold on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X
and S, and the enhanced version for PC.
