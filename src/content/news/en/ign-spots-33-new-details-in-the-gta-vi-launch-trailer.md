IGN has gone through the launch trailer of Grand Theft Auto VI frame by
frame. In a video of 12 minutes, the site lists 33 details. They range from
the army past of Jason to cars, weapons and possible missions.

Most of it is interpretation by IGN, not information from Rockstar Games.
What the trailer itself shows and says is in the
[post about the launch trailer](/news/gta-vi-gets-its-launch-trailer-five-weeks-before-release/).

## Jason and the army

Halfway through the trailer, Jason wears a US Army shirt, IGN notes. The
word Marauder stands out on it. That matches the tattoo of a skull and a
parachute on his arm from earlier videos.

IGN thinks the Marauders are the unit Jason served in. The name may refer to
Merrill's Marauders, an American unit from the Second World War. According
to IGN, Jason was then probably a Ranger.

In the scene where Jason wades through water at night, he wears night vision
goggles and carries a silenced rifle, IGN writes. Two men follow him. A
reptile on the left looks like a Komodo dragon to IGN, an animal from
Indonesia.

## Missions on a large scale

About a minute in, Lucia hangs from a cargo net in black gear, IGN points
out. She fires a Shrewsbury grenade launcher at police cars below. A few
seconds later a helicopter drags a similar net through the side of a tower.

IGN thinks both shots come from the same mission. The glass breaking from
the tower is more detailed than in any earlier GTA, the site writes. Whether
the game has destruction on a larger scale, the shot does not show.

Lucia also jumps from a car onto an airliner. IGN links that to the scene
from the second trailer, where Lucia fights in the hold of a plane.

The bank vault with burning banknotes is the same as on a screenshot that
IGN showed in August. Lucia is there with someone whose face is not visible.
Another shot shows Lucia with gunmen dressed as construction workers.

Whether such partners belong to the story or can be hired, as with the
heists in GTA V, is not known.

## A biplane over the beach

A biplane flies over the beach of Vice City with the logo of a cryptocurrency
on its side. A man on the wing holds a pink smoke flare. IGN suspects that
Jason and Lucia take part in a scam with a fake coin.

## Cars, boats and brands

IGN briefly spots the new Invetero Coquette, with turquoise and pink paint.
The trailer also shows street racing and a chase in a lime green supercar.

Jason and Cal polish a boat that IGN takes for a new model. According to
IGN, Jason works at the start of the story at a marina called Brian's
Marina. A truck of Globe Oil, a brand from GTA IV, drives in the background.

## Characters and gangs

Jason and Lucia stand on the balcony of an apartment with a view of the
beach. IGN calls it a different safehouse than the simpler home from the
extended look.

Cal kisses a pet snake, and an iguana crosses the road. That fits the
[more than 170 animal species](/news/gta-vi-has-more-than-170-animal-species-in-leonida/)
that live in Leonida. Real Dimez and a police officer of Vice City
also appear briefly.

Raul Bautista threatens a man at a construction site while eating chicken.
IGN thinks the bucket comes from Cluckin' Bell, in a new design. Elsewhere
masked people dance around a pile of golden rifles.

## Weapons

Jason puts on brass knuckles and aims a sniper rifle at a helicopter, IGN
notes. An earlier shot shows a helicopter crashing behind him. A man at the
gym shows the
[system for sweat](/news/gta-vi-has-a-scripted-system-that-makes-its-characters-sweat/).

The gold dress of Lucia at the end is the same as in the second trailer. IGN
thinks the scenes belong to one mission, from a yacht to a jump from a roof
into a pool.

## What is not known

Rockstar has not explained any of these scenes. The order of the shots says
nothing about the story, and IGN marks most links as guesses. Grand Theft
Auto VI comes out on 19 November for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S.
