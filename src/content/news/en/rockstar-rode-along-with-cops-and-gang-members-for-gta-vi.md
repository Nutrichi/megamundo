Developers at Rockstar Games travelled through Florida with police officers
and gang members to research Grand Theft Auto VI. Aaron Garbut, co-studio
head and head of development at Rockstar North, says so in issue 27 of LOVE
magazine. The issue reached newsstands on Monday 5 October.

IGN Benelux and Dexerto reported on the interview the same day. It is part
of the long feature on GTA VI that LOVE announced last week with four new
screenshots.

## From Miami to Key West

Every game Rockstar makes starts with research trips, Garbut tells LOVE.
On those trips the team explores both the place and its own ideas as a
group, he says.

For GTA VI, a small group first spent time in Miami and the towns around
it. From there the team drove through the Florida Keys all the way to Key
West. They recorded locations and discussed how to bring them into the
game. Later, larger groups of developers went back to study specific areas
in more detail.

## With cops and with gang members

The team did not only look at buildings and landscapes. According to
Garbut, they spent time with locals and listened to as many stories as
they could. They travelled around with cops and with gang members, he says.

Some of the people they met could be scary, Garbut admits. Yet the people
who showed the team around were among the nicest they had met, according
to Dexerto. The stories, places and details could be terrifying, he says.
IGN Benelux writes that the team also saw the underside of society.

## A day in a helicopter

The research also went up into the air. Garbut spent a full day in a
helicopter with other art directors. They filmed the Everglades, the
mangroves, downtown Miami and the coast.

At one point the helicopter flew low over the mangrove inlets and ended up
hovering above a stadium during a concert by Beyoncé. Garbut thinks they
made the local news because of it, Dexerto reports.

## A place of extremes

For Rockstar, the contrasts within Florida are what matters for GTA VI.
IGN Benelux sums up how the studio describes Miami: a place of extremes,
where great wealth and deep poverty live side by side. Add excess, chaos
and a constant drive to succeed.

The team also studied clothing, haircuts, cosmetics and the different
groups in society. That should make the people of Leonida believable.
Some experiences from the trips ended up in the game in a surprisingly
direct way, according to Rockstar. Which encounters those are is not
known.
