The Pegassi Ignus Pursuit is available in GTA Online. The car is a Law
Enforcement Vehicle and is sold at Warstock Cache & Carry. Rockstar names it
alongside this week's target in The Kortz Center Heist and the general
release of the Grotti Veleno GT.

The Ignus Pursuit is a police version of an existing Pegassi supercar. What
it costs is not in the announcement.

## The heist and the art

This week's primary target in The Kortz Center Heist is a work called
Winter, Nowhere in Particular. Players who reached the Elitist tier of the
Fine Art Collector program can steal it on their first attempt at the heist.

Completing the heist without dying satisfies the Weekly Challenge.
Recovered works can be displayed in a Mansion or sold on.

## The Grotti Veleno GT

The Grotti Veleno GT is now on general sale at Legendary Motorsport. GTA+
members can still claim theirs at no cost at The Vinewood Car Club through
12 August.

Players who qualified for the Elitist tier of the Fine Art Collector program
have until 5 August to claim those rewards.

Salaries for associates and bodyguards pay four times the usual rate this
week.
