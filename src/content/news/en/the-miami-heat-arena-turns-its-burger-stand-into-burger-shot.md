The Kaseya Center, the arena of the Miami Heat, has rebranded its burger restaurant as Burger Shot, the fast-food chain from Grand Theft Auto VI. Posters of Vice City also hang inside the venue. It is part of the marketing in Miami.

The first photos appeared on X on 21 October. The account @videotech shared them and credited the fan accounts @GTAVI_Countdown and @nvtnp_.

## A parody of fast food

In the series, Burger Shot is a parody of chains such as McDonald's and Burger King. Its menu includes The Heartstopper, a burger with seven patties stacked high. Whether the arena also serves a menu in the style of the game is not known. IGN thinks the rebrand is mostly decoration.

## Vice City in the arena

It is not the first GTA VI touch at the Kaseya Center. A [Welcome to Vice City sign of 975,000 dollar](/news/a-vice-city-sign-on-the-miami-heat-arena-costs-975000-dollar/) stands on the roof, and on 18 November the Heat [play as Vice City](/news/the-miami-heat-become-vice-city-on-the-eve-of-gta-vi/), in new jerseys on a court in the style of the game. Earlier this week, [GTA VI beer labels](/news/gta-vi-beer-labels-from-a-florida-brewery-appear-in-a-us-database/) from a brewery near Miami turned up as well. IGN expects that beer to be sold at bars in Miami.

GTA VI comes out on 19 November for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
