Mount Kalaga National Park covers the northern third of Leonida. It is
forest and hill country, with the ridge that gives the park its name as the
highest ground on the map. The elevation shading on the community map puts
that ridge well above anything in the south.

Rockstar has shown little of the area. The second trailer is set in the city
and on the water. What reaches this far north is mostly background: tree
cover, a few dirt roads and the outline of the ridge.

The roads and the smaller place names in the park come from the mapping
community and not from Rockstar. How accurate they are is not known.

Whether the park can be crossed by road from west to east has not been
shown.
