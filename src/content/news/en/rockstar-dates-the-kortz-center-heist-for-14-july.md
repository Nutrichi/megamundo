The Kortz Center Heist comes to GTA Online on 14 July. Players rob an art
museum in Pacific Bluffs. Taking part requires an Art Studio expansion
inside a Mansion property. The heist can be played alone or with three other
players.

The job is handed out by Mr. Faber and his fixer Raf De Angelis. Raf passes
on reconnaissance during the heist, says Rockstar.

## How the robbery works

The Art Studio houses a counterfeiter who makes forgeries of the target
paintings. Gear and technology pile up in the studio during preparation, and
the team picks a loadout from that stock for the finale.

Scoping comes first. Players walk through the gallery, photograph the
paintings without alerting security and look for ways in and out. The museum
also holds secondary targets that can be taken along.

Art that is known to be stolen fetches less on the secondary market,
according to Rockstar. Leaving no witnesses and wiping the CCTV footage
keeps the payout higher.

Stolen paintings do not have to be sold. They can be hung in the Mansion
instead. Three new paintings can be stolen each week.

## What is still unknown

What the Art Studio expansion costs is not stated. How long a full run takes
is not mentioned either. Rockstar says new vehicles arrive with the heist
but does not name them.
