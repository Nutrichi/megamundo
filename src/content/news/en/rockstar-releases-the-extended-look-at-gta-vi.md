Rockstar has released Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look. The video is
available on Netflix, on YouTube and on the official Grand Theft Auto VI
site. Rockstar says the footage was captured entirely in-game on a
PlayStation 5. The game is due on 19 November.

The studio announced the video three weeks earlier and kept to the date it
gave then. The Netflix showing came first, the other platforms followed the
same evening.

## What Rockstar says about the footage

The company states that nothing in the video is a rendered cutscene made
outside the game. That is a claim worth noting, because the material shows
the state of Leonida in more detail than the two trailers did.

Rockstar gives no running time, no list of what is shown and no technical
details in its own announcement. The frame rate is not mentioned either.

## What is still unknown

How much of the video is playable and how much is scripted is not stated.
Whether the footage runs on a standard PlayStation 5 or on the Pro is not
mentioned. Rockstar also says nothing about a version for PC.

Grand Theft Auto VI comes to PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S on
19 November. The game is single player at release.
