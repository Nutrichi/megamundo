Dre'Quan Priest runs the label Only Raw Records together with Boobie Ike.
Rockstar describes him as more of a hustler than a gangster.

Even when he was dealing on the street to get by, music was the goal,
according to Rockstar. Until now he books acts for the strip club of Boobie.

With the signing of the rap duo Real Dimez, he is now aiming at the music
scene of Vice City. How far he gets is not known.
