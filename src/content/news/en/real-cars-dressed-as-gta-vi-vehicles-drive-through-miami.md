Real cars made up as vehicles from Grand Theft Auto VI drove through Miami
this week. They carried license plates of Leonida. Among them was a
Lamborghini in the colours of the Vice City police, IGN writes.

The convoy is part of the marketing of Rockstar Games for the game in
Miami, according to IGN.

## Which cars

The cars drove close together through the city, IGN writes. Next to the
police Lamborghini there was a McLaren dressed up as a Progen Emerus. Real
versions of the Cheetah, the Coquette D10, the Tempesta and the Manana also
drove along.

The local news page Only in Dade saw some of the cars at a gas station on
15 October.

## Reactions

Reactions on social media are mostly positive, IGN writes. Players praise
the creativity of the campaign. One user on X called the police Lamborghini
"FIRE".

## Part of a larger campaign

The convoy follows the
[sign on the arena of the Miami Heat](/news/a-vice-city-sign-on-the-miami-heat-arena-costs-975000-dollar/),
ads in the stadium of the Dolphins and the blimp. Some officials of
Miami-Dade County object to the campaign. The county commission meets again
on 20 October.

## What is not known

Whether Rockstar has confirmed the convoy, IGN does not say. How much the cars cost,
and whether they will drive elsewhere, is not known. Grand Theft Auto VI comes
out on 19 November for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S.
