Grand Theft Auto VI is on the cover of Game Informer. Issue 382 carries a
cover story of 14 pages about Vice City and Leonida, with 12 new screenshots.
The digital edition appears on 29 September.

The cover shows Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos in a red convertible, with the
skyline of Vice City behind them. The artwork is original work by Rockstar
Games, writes Game Informer, which revealed the cover on 25 September.

## What the story covers

Writer Marcus Stewart, who grew up in South Florida, spoke with several lead
designers at Rockstar. The story focuses on how Rockstar brought Leonida, its
parody of Florida, and Vice City to life.

Game Informer names three subjects that Rockstar had not shared before. The
weather systems simulate the less sunny side of Florida. Wildlife, native and
not, roams Leonida on land, in the air and at sea. And the story explains how
Rockstar researched Miami and Florida for the world.

What those details are exactly, the announcement does not say. They are in
the magazine itself, and so are the 12 screenshots.

## When and where

The digital version appears on 29 September, according to Game Informer.
The print edition reaches subscribers and bookshops in the coming weeks. A
subscription covers 10 issues a year and ships to more than 50 countries. The
issue is only available for a limited time, the magazine writes.

Grand Theft Auto VI appears on 19 November for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X
and S.
