IGN has published a video of 39 minutes that puts the whole Grand Theft
Auto series in one timeline. Rockstar Games keeps the 2D, 3D and HD
universes officially apart. IGN blends them anyway, and ends with GTA VI in
Vice City.

## The rule behind the timeline

IGN treats the events of the 2D and 3D games as valid, unless an HD game
contradicts them. That way the video keeps what fits and drops what does
not. The reason is the marketing of GTA VI, according to IGN. It is full of
nods to the Vice City of 1986, such as the Vercetti estate and Phil Cassidy.

The video is in 19 chapters, from the 1960s to 2026. It is also on the
[YouTube channel of IGN](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MzjbfNfx1Kw).

## From London to Vice City

The timeline starts in the 1960s, with the London games of the 2D era. Then
the video moves to the three cities that Rockstar keeps returning to:
Liberty City, San Andreas and Vice City.

In 1984, Victor Vance builds up the drug trade of Vice City, in Vice City
Stories. In 1986, Tommy Vercetti comes down from Liberty City for the
Forelli family. He later takes over the mansion of Ricardo Diaz. That house
still carries the name Vercetti estate 40 years later, IGN notes.

## The fall of the Forelli family

The thread through the 3D era is the decline of the Forelli family,
according to IGN. In San Andreas, set in 1992, CJ kills Marco Forelli in
Liberty City for Salvatore Leone. In Liberty City Stories, set in 1998, Toni
Cipriani helps Leone in his war against the Forellis.

In 2001, GTA III follows Claude, who already met CJ in San Andreas as a
rival for Catalina. Claude kills Salvatore Leone at the end. With that, IGN
closes the era of the Italian mob in Liberty City.

## Lucia in the timeline

Around the same time, IGN places the birth of Lucia Caminos in Liberty
City. The video gives no exact year. Rockstar itself writes that her
family once lived in Liberty City. Her father taught her to fight at a young
age, as the [page about Lucia](/game/characters/lucia-caminos/) describes.

## The HD era

In 2004, the robbery in Ludendorff goes wrong for Michael, Trevor and Brad.
Michael fakes his death and starts over in Los Santos. In 2008, Niko Bellic
arrives in Liberty City for GTA IV. A year later, Huang Lee follows in
Chinatown Wars.

The longest chapter covers GTA V, set in 2013, with Michael, Franklin and
Trevor. IGN points to Lazlow as one of the links between the universes.
Lazlow is on the radio in the Vice City era and hosts a talent show in GTA V.

## GTA VI

GTA VI is the fourth visit to Vice City since 1997, IGN counts. Lucia lives
there with Jason, her partner in crime and in life. According to IGN, the
two carry out a big robbery in Port Gellhorn.

Phil Cassidy may be the first character to appear in person in both the 3D
and HD universes, according to IGN. He looks the same in 2026 as in 1986,
and seems to have his lost arm back. IGN concludes that it is not exactly the same
character.

The video does not change the official line of Rockstar. IGN calls its own
timeline a unifying theory. In October, IGN also
[traced the cinematic style of GTA VI](/news/ign-traces-the-cinematic-style-of-gta-vi-back-to-san-andreas/)
back to San Andreas.

## What is not known

Details of the story of GTA VI cannot be confirmed before release, IGN says.
Whether Lucia was born in Liberty City, Rockstar does not say. Grand
Theft Auto VI comes out on 19 November for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X
and S.
