Players can claim a Hands On Car Wash in GTA Online at no cost. The property
is at Maze Bank Foreclosures through 14 January. It belongs to the Money
Fronts business. Legal fronts pay triple rewards this week.

Money Fronts are legal businesses used to launder money from criminal
activity. The car wash is the entry point to that portfolio. Claiming it
requires having played the introductory cutscene first.

## Bonuses this week

Legal fronts pay three times the usual GTA$ and RP. Completing three car
wash jobs satisfies the Weekly Challenge.

Owners of a Counterfeit Cash Factory earn extra from their biker business or
nightclub. The factory properties themselves are 40 percent off, along with
their upgrades.

The Hao's Special Works race series pays four times the usual rate through
14 January. Those races run only on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X and S, and
the Enhanced version on PC.

The Grotti LSCM Cheetah Classic is 30 percent off at Legendary Motorsport
this week.

## What is still unknown

Rockstar says three new Odd Jobs arrive later in January but does not name
them. An exact date is not given either.
