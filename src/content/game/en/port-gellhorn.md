Port Gellhorn lies on the northwest coast, on the stretch of Leonida that
corresponds to the Florida panhandle. The real-world reference is Panama
City. After Vice City it is the second built-up area on the map.

The town is laid out around a working port. The buildings are low and the
street grid runs out into sand and scrub rather than into suburbs. That
makes it a different kind of place from Vice City, which sits on the other
side of the state.

The town and its name are among the better established parts of the map. The
airfield to the south and the road that runs east from it come from the
mapping community, according to the legend on the community map.

What the port is used for in the game has not been shown.
