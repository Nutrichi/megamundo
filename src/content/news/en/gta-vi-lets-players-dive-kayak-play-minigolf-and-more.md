Grand Theft Auto VI has plenty to do outside a life of crime. Players can
dive, kayak and play minigolf, according to the cover story of Game Informer.
Gyms, races and zoos are on the list too.

## On the water

Much of it happens on and around the water. Besides kayaking and diving, the
magazine names jet skis and airboats. An airboat is a flat boat with a large
propeller at the back, known from the swamps of Florida.

In reality those swamps are the Everglades. A version of them is in GTA VI as
well, writes IGN Benelux.

## On land

On land, players can play minigolf and billiards and work out in gyms. There
are street races and offroad races for those who prefer to drive. Base jumping
is on the list, and so is a visit to the zoo.

## The full list

- Base jumping
- Billiards
- Working out in gyms
- Kayaking
- Minigolf
- Diving
- Street and offroad races
- Visiting zoos
- Airboat rides
- Jet skiing

The list covers only part of what the game offers, according to IGN Benelux.
What else there is to do, Rockstar has not said yet.

Grand Theft Auto VI appears on 19 November for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X
and S.
