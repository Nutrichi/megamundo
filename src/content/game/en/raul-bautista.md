Raul Bautista is an experienced bank robber. Rockstar describes him as
confident, charming and cunning.

He is always looking for talent willing to take the risks that bring the
biggest rewards. According to Rockstar, his recklessness raises the stakes
with every job.

Sooner or later his crew will have to choose between doubling down and
getting out, Rockstar writes. Who belongs to that crew is not known.
