GTA+ members can claim the Pfister Astrale from 8 January. The sports
classic is free at The Vinewood Car Club and stays exclusive to members
until 4 February. Rockstar releases the car to everyone after that date.

The Astrale on display comes in a livery that is also sold at participating
auto shops and vehicle workshops.

## The rest of the month for members

Cream Pearl Chameleon Paint and the matching wheel paint are free at all
auto shops until 4 February. The Pfister Chore Jacket goes into member
wardrobes automatically this month.

Auto Shop Robbery Contract finales pay double GTA$ and RP. Auto Shop client
jobs pay double GTA$ and LS Car Meet Rep, also until 4 February. Missions in
Operation Paper Trail pay double GTA$ and RP all month.

Members receive a monthly payment into their Maze Bank account on billing,
and can buy member-only Shark Cards with bonus cash. The amounts are not in
the announcement.

## What is still unknown

Rockstar gives no price for the Astrale after 4 February and no performance
figures. Where the car goes on sale once it leaves The Vinewood Car Club is
not stated either.
