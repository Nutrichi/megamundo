Stephen Bliss, who helped shape the look of Grand Theft Auto, is flattered by
the cover of GTA VI. The grid of boxes and the helicopter in the top left
corner go back to the work of Bliss. Bliss told IGN that at Lucca Comics and
Games.

Bliss worked at Rockstar Games from 2001 to 2016. The artist had no part in the
cover of GTA VI, and says so clearly. The influence is limited to small
references, according to Bliss, and the cover speaks a language of its own.

## The grid and the comic book style

Bliss helped set the style of the promotional artwork of the series, IGN
writes. That style leans on comic books and uses the grid that has become the
trademark of GTA. It moves Bliss that this heritage lives on.

Bliss also points to other artists at Rockstar, such as Anthony Macbain and
Steven Olds. Bliss does not claim a large share in the new cover.

## The longer interview

In the full interview with IGN, Bliss also talks about the cover of GTA III and
how the attacks of 11 September 2001 influenced it. The styles of Vice City
and San Andreas come up too.

Who designed the cover of GTA VI, IGN does not say. Grand Theft Auto VI comes
out on 19 November for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S.
