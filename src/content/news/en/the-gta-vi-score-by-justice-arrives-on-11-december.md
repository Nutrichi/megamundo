Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score arrives on 11 December on all major
streaming services. It also comes on double CD and triple vinyl that day. The
French electronic duo Justice made the music. A new track, "Pusher", is out
now.

Rockstar Games announced the date on 29 October, IGN writes. The score comes
three weeks after the game and after
[Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album](/news/rockstar-announces-the-gta-vi-album-with-34-songs-on-cd-for-17-euro/),
the soundtrack with songs by other artists.

## Thirty tracks and three hours

The score holds 30 tracks and a number of digital bonus tracks, about three
hours of music from the game. The bonus tracks are part of every format. The
vinyl comes in standard black and in a limited crystal clear edition.

The single "Flower in a Hurricane", with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard &
The Lizard Wizard, is on it too. Justice released it with
[the launch trailer](/news/gta-vi-gets-its-launch-trailer-five-weeks-before-release/).
It reached number 62 in the Billboard Hot 100.

## Pusher with Gesaffelstein

"Pusher" gives a first taste of the instrumental side of the score. It is the
first collaboration between Justice and Gesaffelstein, another French
electronic artist.

## Music that reacts to play

Justice is Gaspard Augé and Xavier de Rosnay. The duo worked closely with
Rockstar, and the score adapts to what happens in the game. The game is so
broad in places and themes that the music had to cover many emotions, genres
and textures, de Rosnay says.

Prices for the CD and the vinyl are not known. Grand Theft Auto VI comes out on
19 November for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S.
