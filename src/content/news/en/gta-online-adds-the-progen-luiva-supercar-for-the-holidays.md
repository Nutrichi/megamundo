The Progen Luiva is now in GTA Online. The supercar has no roof, no
windshield and no windows, which Rockstar presents as a deliberate choice.
It is sold at Legendary Motorsport and at the Luxury Autos display.

GTA+ members can also pick it up at The Vinewood Car Club showroom on
Elysian Island. No price is given.

## The Vapid FMJ MK V

The Vapid FMJ MK V is now on general sale at Legendary Motorsport and Luxury
Autos. GTA+ members can still claim it free at The Vinewood Car Club through
7 January. On PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X and S and PC Enhanced it can be
upgraded further.

Players who finished all three New Listings Missions before 7 December have
until 7 January to collect their Gold Tier rewards. Those include a discount
on Prix Luxury Real Estate properties and the Black Rockstar Varsity
Crewneck.

## Holiday content

Festive clothing, trees and decorations have appeared across Southern San
Andreas. Rockstar says snow is forecast later in the month.

Logging in between 23 December and 7 January gives gifts, including the
Season's Greetings Sweater and the New Year Fireworks Bodysuit. Holiday
modes return between 23 and 29 December.

A free Buckingham SuperVolito Carbon helicopter can be claimed from Elitas
Travel between 23 and 29 December.
