GTA+ members can claim the Dewbauchee Champion in GTA Online at no cost. The
supercar is at The Vinewood Car Club showroom and can be claimed through
4 March. Members also get the Grotti Itali Classic a week before general
release.

The Champion is a supercar with weapons, Rockstar writes. The Itali Classic
is a sports classic from the 1980s in the Grotti range.

## Early access and the member discount

The Itali Classic stands on the showroom floor for test drives during the
early access week. Members buy it with the usual 20 percent member discount.

## What else members get this month

The Pink Prismatic Pearl Chameleon Paint and the matching wheel paints are
free at all auto shops through 4 March. The LS Golf Club Varsity is added to
member wardrobes.

An Agency property is discounted for members at the Dynasty 8 Executive
site, along with its upgrades and modifications.

Security Contracts pay double GTA$ and RP. Stunt races pay double as well,
including the Lunar New Year stunt races that run from 12 February to
4 March.

## What is still unknown

What the Itali Classic costs after the early access week is not stated.
