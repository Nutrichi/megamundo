The GTA VI leaker CyberLeek threatens to release a build of the game with the
full story mode. That would happen once the memecoin of the leaker is worth
30 million dollars, IGN reports. At the time the coin stood at 3.8 million.

## What the message says

The warning appeared on the website of CyberLeek, according to online reports
cited by IGN. The build would hold the full story mode, played to the end and
including the end credits. Take-Two can stop it with a disc version of the
game and a public apology.

CyberLeek again presents the leaks as a protest against the lack of a
physical disc for Grand Theft Auto VI. Whether the leaker means a playable
build or a video of a full playthrough is not clear, IGN notes.

## Far from the target

Market value is the total value of all coins of a crypto asset in
circulation. When IGN published its article, the coin of CyberLeek was worth
3.8 million dollars. Earlier that day it peaked at 5.7 million, as the message
spread on social media. IGN calls 30 million unlikely.

## Targets tied to Lucia

CyberLeek also tied lower targets to the clothing of Lucia in new leaked
footage, IGN writes. At 2 million dollars the leaker promised footage of
Lucia in a bikini. At 4 million that would be footage of Lucia fully naked.
Whether such footage has appeared is not known.

Many fans doubt that the leaker cares about physical media. They accuse
CyberLeek of running a crypto scam, and criticise anyone who buys the coin,
IGN reports.

## Back after a quiet period

CyberLeek went quiet after a series of leaks in August. Take-Two, the parent
company of Rockstar Games, started an investigation and sent subpoenas to
companies such as Discord. This week the leaker returned with an alleged
gameplay video of 24 minutes, according to IGN. It shows cutscenes, story
moments, a first-person camera, and Jason and Lucia naked.

Every video of CyberLeek points to a website where fans pay with the coin to
vote on new leaks. Such a vote is reportedly running now, IGN writes. An
analysis of crypto transactions in late August suggested the leaker had
cashed out around 270,000 dollars.

According to Rockstar, the publicity around the CyberLeek hack wiped around
2 billion dollars off the market value of Take-Two. The studio said so at the
[employment tribunal in Glasgow](/news/rockstar-compares-its-security-around-gta-vi-to-a-wall-street-bank/).

## What is not known

Whether CyberLeek really has such a build is not known. How the footage got
past the security of Rockstar is not known either, and neither is who is
behind the leaks. In August Rockstar called the leaks heartbreaking for its
developers and unfortunate for fans. The studio kept the release date of
19 November.
