GTA+ members can claim the Bravado Buffalo STX Pursuit free from 2 April.
The law enforcement vehicle stands at The Vinewood Car Club and at Warstock
Cache & Carry. Everyone else gets access on 9 April, says Rockstar.

Members pay 20 percent less on the other law enforcement vehicles at The
Vinewood Car Club. Which vehicles those are is not listed in the
announcement.

## The rest of the month for members

NBA 2K26 stays in the GTA+ Games Library until 20 April, on PlayStation 5
and Xbox Series X and S. After that date the game leaves the library.

Light Green Pearl Chameleon Paint and the matching wheel paint are free at
all vehicle workshops. The Bigness Crowned Varsity Jacket and Wide Pants go
into member wardrobes automatically.

Dispatch Work pays a bonus this month. Bail Office bounties, wildlife
photography and Madrazo Hits all pay double GTA$ and RP. Ammunition and body
armour are half price at participating stores.

## What is still unknown

Rockstar gives no price for the Buffalo STX Pursuit after 9 April. Whether
the bonuses run to the end of the month or stop earlier is not stated for
every one of them.
