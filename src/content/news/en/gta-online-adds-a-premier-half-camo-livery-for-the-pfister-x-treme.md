GTA Online has a new livery for the Pfister X-Treme. The Premier Half Camo
wrap is available at all Vehicle Workshops. Salvage Yards pay double daily
income this week and scrap vehicles twice as fast.

Rockstar does not say what the livery costs.

## Salvage Yard week

Two Robbery Target Vehicles can be claimed by finishing Salvage Yard
Robberies. Both come with a limited-time vanity plate, LS Pounders and LS
Panic. Rockstar does not name the two cars in plain text.

Completing any two Salvage Yard Robberies satisfies the Weekly Challenge.
Salvage Yard owners with a Tow Truck earn double on towing services.

## Races and what comes next

The Community Race Series pays double this week. A new stunt track that
circles the Vinewood Reservoir and Blaine County has been added to it.

Rockstar says next week brings an event around the best player-made Jobs and
the Creators behind them. Which jobs return is not stated.

GTA+ members can claim a free Off-Road vehicle at The Vinewood Car Club
through 1 April. The Broker Prolaps basketball top and shorts are added to
member wardrobes.
