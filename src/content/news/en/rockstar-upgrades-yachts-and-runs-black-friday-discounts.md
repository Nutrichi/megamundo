Owners of a Galaxy Super Yacht in GTA Online can upgrade to the most
expensive model at no cost. On PC the upgrade costs GTA$1. Rockstar is also
running Black Friday discounts from 27 November to 3 December. A Superyacht
Life missions pay extra this week.

The upgrade applies to existing yacht owners. Players without a yacht get a
discount on the Galaxy models at DockTease.

## This week's bonuses

Completing three A Superyacht Life missions satisfies the Weekly Challenge.
The reward is a smoking jacket.

This week's FIB Priority File pays four times the usual rate. Owners of a
Garment Factory launch those files from an app on the in-game phone.

The Community Combat Series pays triple rewards.

## Black Friday

Rockstar calls the vehicle discounts some of the highest it has offered.
Selected properties are half price. The window runs from 27 November to
3 December.

Players who complete a set of missions by 7 December move up to a higher
tier with Prix Luxury Real Estate, which locks in a preferred rate.

## What is still unknown

Whether the yacht upgrade stays available after this week is not stated.
