Rockstar has added the Gallivanter Warden to GTA Online. The SUV is free to
claim for GTA+ members at The Vinewood Car Club showroom. Members get it a
week before other players. The claim window runs through 9 September.

Rockstar describes the Warden as a balance between comfort and off-road
capability. Members can also claim Light Purple Pearl Chameleon paint for
bodywork and rims, free at all Vehicle Workshops.

## The rest of the week

The Darnell Bros Garment Factory is 60% off at Maze Bank Foreclosures. The
FIB Files missions pay double GTA$ and RP. Terrorbyte Bureau tech is half
price.

Drift Races pay triple GTA$, RP and LS Car Meet reputation. Rockstar says
that rate stacks to six times between 27 August and 2 September. Drift
Tuning upgrades are half price for the same period.

Two clothing items from a Sessanta Nove and Princess Robot Bubblegum
collaboration are added to members' wardrobes.

## What is still unknown

Rockstar does not name the date on which the Warden becomes available to all
players. The announcement only says one week. A price for the vehicle is not
given either.
