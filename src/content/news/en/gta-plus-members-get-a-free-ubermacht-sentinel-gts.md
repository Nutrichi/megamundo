GTA+ members can claim the Übermacht Sentinel GTS in GTA Online at no cost.
The sports car is at The Vinewood Car Club showroom and at Legendary
Motorsport. The offer runs through 10 June. Members also get free paints and
discounts on Executive Office properties.

The Sentinel GTS is an import, Rockstar writes, and sits in the sports
class.

## What else members get this month

The Magenta and Cyan Flip Chameleon Paint and the matching wheel paint are
free at all vehicle workshops. The Junk x Jackal Racing gear is added to
member wardrobes automatically, and a Coil Junk Energy Inductor can be
claimed at Pedal and Metal Cycles.

Executive Office properties and their upgrades are discounted for members
through 10 June.

## Bonuses this month

Import and export sales pay double GTA$ and RP. Special Vehicle Work,
launched from the computer in an Executive Office, pays double as well.

## What is still unknown

Rockstar does not say whether the Sentinel GTS goes on general sale later,
or at what price. Claim windows for members have in the past run a month
ahead of general release, but the announcement makes no promise.
