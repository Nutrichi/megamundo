Rockstar is running Motor Madness in GTA Online. The event lasts two weeks,
from 14 to 27 May. The first week centres on Salvage Yards, the second on
the LS Car Meet. Membership of the car meet is free during the event.

In the first week, Salvage Yard robbery sales and salvage value pay double.
Simeon contact missions pay triple GTA$ and RP.

## The second week

From 21 May the event moves to the racing scene. Auto Shop robbery finales
pay double. The Community Race Series, built from player-made tracks, pays
double GTA$ and RP.

Players who are not yet members can get a free LS Car Meet membership from
Mimi inside the venue, Rockstar says.

## Liveries, suits and showrooms

A new livery for the Vapid FMJ MK V is available at all Vehicle Workshops.
Blue and Yellow Tire Smoke is half price.

Two weekly challenges award Manor racing suits. The first asks for two race
wins between 14 and 20 May, the second for two LS Car Meet race wins between
21 and 27 May.

Buying a vehicle on display at Simeon's Premium Deluxe Motorsport, Luxury
Autos or the LS Car Meet earns a separate reward. Rockstar says those arrive
within 72 hours of the purchase.
