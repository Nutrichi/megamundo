GTA Online has three new missions called New Listings. They prepare the
arrival of mansion properties in December. Completing all three before 7
December earns a discount on those homes. The missions pay a bonus in the
meantime.

The properties come from Prix Luxury Real Estate, a new company in the Los
Santos property market. Mr. Faber sits in the consortium behind the
development, according to Rockstar.

## What the missions involve

The three jobs are clearing out squatters, procuring construction materials
and motivating contractors. Together they make up the entry to the Prix
Luxury VIP programme.

Anyone who plays between 13 November and 7 December also qualifies for a free
sports car with the Sessanta Nove Monogram livery. It is available at Luxury
Autos in Rockford Hills. A set of clothing items comes with it.

Alongside this, executive work pays a bonus until 19 November. The Lombank
West Executive Office is free this week and the other offices are discounted,
as are their upgrades and garage expansions.

## What is still unknown

What the mansions will cost is not stated. The exact date in December on
which they go on sale is not given either.
