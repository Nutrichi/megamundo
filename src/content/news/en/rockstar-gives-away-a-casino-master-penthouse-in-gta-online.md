Rockstar is giving away the Master Penthouse above The Diamond Casino and
Resort this week. Casino story missions and casino work pay double GTA$ and
RP. The daily allowance of chips at the cashier is doubled. The Diamond
Casino Heist finale also pays double.

Rockstar frames the week as preparation for luxury property arriving later.
The penthouse sits directly above the casino floor.

## Upgrades and the property ladder

A set of penthouse upgrades is free for GTA+ members, among them an extra
bedroom, a lounge, a bar and a spa. On PC in the Enhanced version each
upgrade costs a nominal 1 GTA$.

The New Listings missions pay bonus GTA$ and RP. Reaching them requires
meeting Raf and Mr. Faber through Martin Madrazo at the airport. After that
the Prix Luxury Real Estate icon appears in the Tongva Hills.

## The VIP tiers

Players at Black tier in the Very Important Prix Program can claim rewards at
the Luxury Autos showroom in Rockford Hills. Completing all three New
Listings missions by 7 December reaches the next tier, which brings a
discount on the mansion properties still to come.

Sixteen vehicles are 30 percent off, many of them cars that arrived when the
casino first opened.
