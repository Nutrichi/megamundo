Owners of the Hands On Car Wash in GTA Online can claim a second business
for free. It is the Higgins Helitours front, available at Maze Bank
Foreclosures until 3 June. Money laundering missions pay four times the
usual amount in the same week.

The fronts exist to hide criminal income. Laundering missions at the Hands
On Car Wash, at Higgins Helitours and at the Smoke on the Water dispensary
all pay the higher rate.

## How the fronts work

Laundered income is collected from a safe inside the Hands On Car Wash.
Balancing criminal earnings against legitimate business at each front keeps
the Heat down, according to Rockstar.

The Hands On Car Wash is the entry point to the system and is discounted
this week. The Smoke on the Water dispensary is discounted as well.

The weekly challenge asks for three Hands On Car Wash Legal Missions.

Work for Mr. Faber pays double, and Lamar contact missions pay a bonus. The
Community Combat Series also pays double and features a team deathmatch in
which Ramp Buggies launch opposing vehicles into the air.

## What is still unknown

Whether Higgins Helitours stays free after 3 June is not stated. How much
passive income the business generates is not mentioned either.
