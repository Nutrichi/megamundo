Rockstar is giving away the Lago Zancudo Bunker in GTA Online. The property
is free from Maze Bank Foreclosures through 13 July. The studio calls the
accompanying sale its biggest ever. It covers aircraft, weaponized vehicles
and older cars.

Players who prefer a different location get a discount on the other Bunker
properties and on their upgrades. Bunker research and supply missions pay
extra during the event.

## Racing, battles and a deadline

The Stunt Race Series pays triple GTA$ and RP. The Community Series, a mix
of rally races, duels and a musket deathmatch, pays double.

The Fine Art Collector Program closes on 13 July. Playing GTA Online before
that date confirms a player's tier, Rockstar says. Completing a heist raises
that tier, and Mansion owners reach the highest level by playing.

## An offer on Shark Cards

Rockstar is running a limited offer of 40% bonus GTA$ on Shark Cards, which
are bought with real money. Players opt in through 22 July and have until
5 August to buy. The offer is limited to five redemptions per card type.

## Action against voice chat

Rockstar writes that it has partnered with Modulate to act against abusive
voice chat. The studio also promises unspecified improvements when The Kortz
Center Heist arrives later in July. What those improvements are is not
stated.
