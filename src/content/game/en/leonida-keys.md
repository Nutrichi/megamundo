The Leonida Keys are a chain of small islands running southwest from the
southern tip of the state, linked by bridges. The real-world reference is
the Florida Keys, and the shape follows it closely.

The islands are small, so what stands on them matters more than how large
they are. The community map marks a marina and an airstrip on the largest of
them.

Whether the chain can be driven from end to end has not been shown. Neither
has how the bridges behave in the game.

The Keys appear only briefly in the material released so far.
