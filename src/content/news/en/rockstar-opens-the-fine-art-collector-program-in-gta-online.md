Rockstar has opened the Fine Art Collector Program in GTA Online. It runs
from 18 June to 13 July and prepares players for The Kortz Center Heist. The
programme has three tiers and pays up to GTA$1,500,000 in bonus cash.

Anyone who plays between those dates reaches the first tier and gets an
armoured sedan from Warstock. The car seats five and carries a
roof-mounted Minigun.

## Three tiers

Completing any GTA Online heist between 18 June and 13 July unlocks the
second tier. The reward for it is not named in plain text in the
announcement.

The third tier is for mansion owners. They get an upgrade for their mansion
that is needed to start The Kortz Center Heist, plus a helicopter from
Warstock.

Rewards for the first two tiers arrive within 72 hours. The third tier pays
out once The Kortz Center Heist launches in July.

## The rest of the week

The finale of The Diamond Casino Heist is guaranteed to pay double when the
vault holds art. Casino story missions and Casino Work pay double GTA$ and
RP.

An arcade in Grapeseed is free this week. A mansion in Vinewood Hills comes
with a GTA$2,000,000 discount for programme members.
