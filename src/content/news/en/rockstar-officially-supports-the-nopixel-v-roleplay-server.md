Rockstar is officially supporting nopixel V, a roleplay server for Grand
Theft Auto V. The closed beta starts on 8 September and runs through the
Rockstar Games Launcher. Rockstar calls it the next evolution of the GTAV
Roleplay community.

GTA Roleplay turns Grand Theft Auto V into a community-run version of Los
Santos. Players create their own characters and tell their own stories in a
shared world. A player-run police force keeps the criminals in check.

## What nopixel V changes

The server has a reworked character customisation system, according to
Rockstar. New parts of the city include the Pixel Hotel and Casino and a
rebuilt six-block stretch of Cypress Flats. Rockstar also mentions refreshed
visuals, more ways to earn money on both sides of the law, and pedestrians
that react more.

Watching streamers play nopixel V on Twitch between 8 and 30 September earns
Twitch Drops. Those include the Burger Shot Tracksuit and GTA$1,500,000 in
GTA Online.

## What is still unknown

Rockstar does not say who gets into the closed beta or how many players it
holds. When the server opens to everyone is not stated either. The
announcement mentions later updates, including a new in-game overlay for PC,
but gives no dates.
