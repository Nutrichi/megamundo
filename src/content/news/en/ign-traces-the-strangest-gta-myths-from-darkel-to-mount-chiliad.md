IGN has made a video of 19 minutes about the strangest myths in Grand Theft
Auto. It runs from cut content in GTA III to the mystery of Mount Chiliad in
GTA V. At the end, IGN looks at what that means for GTA VI.

The video appeared on 29 October and is also on the
[YouTube channel of IGN](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2wNTRGJr_NY).

## Darkel and the Dodo

GTA III came out after a delay of three weeks, following the attacks of
11 September 2001. Rockstar Games changed about 1 percent of the game,
according to IGN. Fans long wondered what else had been removed.

The Dodo, a plane with clipped wings, was one suspect. A design document shows
that it never had to fly, IGN says: a plane that flies would outrun how fast
the PlayStation 2 could load the world. Darkel, a character who handed out odd
jobs, was cut well before the attacks. A name in the credits and an unused
texture remain.

## Bigfoot in San Andreas

Stories about Bigfoot in San Andreas went round within days of the release in
2004. The Hot Coffee affair, with a hidden sex minigame on the disc, made fans
believe that Rockstar buries secrets. Co-founder Terry Donovan said there is
no Bigfoot. Modders never found a model in the files either.

Rockstar did play along. Undead Nightmare for Red Dead Redemption has several
Sasquatches. In GTA V, Franklin meets a hunter who has chased Bigfoot for
years.

## Ratman in GTA IV

In GTA IV, the myth moved to the subway of Liberty City. Forum threads about
Ratman appeared within weeks of the release, with orange puddles and strange
squeaks as clues. Ratman was never found in the game or its files. A wiki on
GTA myths, founded in 2012, now holds about 1,500 pages.

## Mount Chiliad

The mural at the top of Mount Chiliad in GTA V shows a UFO, an egg and a figure
with a jetpack. After 100 percent completion, a UFO appears there at 3:00 in
the rain. The Doomsday Heist of 2017 in GTA Online revealed a base inside the
mountain and a working jetpack.

## And GTA VI

The hunt now turns to leaks before a game comes out, rather than secrets
inside it, IGN concludes. What GTA VI hides is not known. The game comes out
on 19 November for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S.
