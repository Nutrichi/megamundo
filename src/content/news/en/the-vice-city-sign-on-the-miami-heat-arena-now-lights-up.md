The "Welcome to Vice City" sign on the Kaseya Center in Miami is now lit. The
lettering on the roof of the arena ends in the Roman numerals VI. Rockstar
Games uses it to promote Grand Theft Auto VI.

Reddit user Ground_Breaker49 posted a video of the lit sign on r/GTA on
1 October. The aerial footage shows the arena by the water at night, with the
lettering glowing on the dark roof. The image at the top is a still from that
video.

## The Sahara Arena in the game

In GTA VI the building is called the Sahara Arena, IGN writes. It is clearly
visible in one of the new screenshots that Game Informer published on
29 September. As in Miami, a Ferris wheel stands next to the arena by the
water.

The Kaseya Center is the home of NBA team Miami Heat and stands on Biscayne
Bay. Rockstar pays 975,000 dollar for the sign, around 840,000 euro. The
money goes to Miami-Dade County, which owns the arena.

## Months of talks with the county

Rockstar had been talking to Miami-Dade County for months about a large
campaign in the city, according to IGN. Several ideas came up, among them
taking over parts of the airport, drone shows and events on the beaches. In
the end the choice fell on the Kaseya Center. Whether any of the other ideas
still go ahead is not known.

## A split vote in Miami Beach

The neighbouring city of Miami Beach approved a separate campaign on
10 September. The city commission voted 4 to 3 in favour, IGN reported at the
time. Rockstar pays the city at least 3 million dollar. Branding for the game
appears there from 15 October to 31 December.

Commissioner David Suarez voted against. During a meeting he showed footage
from the extended GTA VI trailer and said the game does not match the values
of the community. Commissioner Monica Matteo-Salinas voted in favour. She
called the campaign a win for gamers, herself included.

GTA VI is due on 19 November for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S.
