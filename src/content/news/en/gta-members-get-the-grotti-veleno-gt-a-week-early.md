GTA+ members can claim the Grotti Veleno GT for free from The Vinewood Car
Club. The supercar is theirs a week before it reaches Legendary Motorsport
for everyone else. The offer runs through 12 August.

The car comes wrapped in the Attack livery. Rockstar does not say what the
Veleno GT costs once it goes on general sale.

## Discounts aimed at the new heist

Most of this week's offers point at The Kortz Center Heist, which needs a
Mansion with an Art Studio. GTA+ members get GTA$1,000,000 off the Art
Studio through 12 August. A discount of GTA$2,000,000 on any Mansion from
Prix Luxury Real Estate runs through 5 August.

There is also 50 percent off Hao's Special Works upgrades and off vehicle
conversions at Benny's Original Motor Works.

## Free items for members

The Black Oil Spill Prismatic Chameleon paint and the matching wheel paint
are free at participating Auto Shops. The Orange Manor Bomber Zipped jacket
is added to member wardrobes.

HSW Time Trials pay double rewards through 12 August. Between 14 and 22 July
that stacks to four times the usual payout.
