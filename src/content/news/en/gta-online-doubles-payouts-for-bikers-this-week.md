Rockstar is doubling payouts for biker businesses in GTA Online. Street
dealer sales pay double GTA$ this week. Clubhouse contracts pay more, and
counterfeit cash factories are discounted. The same week marks the Lunar New
Year.

Counterfeit cash factories and their upgrades are cheaper for the duration.
Rockstar does not say how large the discount is.

## Lunar New Year

Stunt races decorated for the Year of the Horse pay triple GTA$ and RP.
Winning two of them completes the weekly challenge.

Tattoo artists across the city are giving away horse tattoos. Horse masks
are free at Vespucci Movie Masks and Memorabilia on the Vespucci Beach
Boardwalk.

Rockstar has hidden 36 collectibles around Los Santos. Each one pays GTA$
and RP, and collecting all 36 pays a bonus of GTA$88,888.

## Community series

The Community Combat Series pays double GTA$ and RP. The newest entry is a
Team King of the Hill played on a pyramid built in the Grand Senora Desert.

How long the biker bonuses run is not stated in the announcement. Rockstar
only calls it this week.
