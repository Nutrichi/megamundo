Lucia Caminos is the second main character of Grand Theft Auto VI, next to
Jason Duval. According to Rockstar, her father taught her to fight almost as
soon as she could walk.

Fighting for her family landed her in the Leonida Penitentiary, Rockstar
writes, and luck got her out again. She has decided to make only smart moves
from now on.

Her mother has dreamed of a better life since the family lived in Liberty
City. Lucia wants that life, and according to Rockstar she is prepared to
take it herself. A life with Jason could be her way out.

What she was convicted of and how long she spent in prison is not known.
