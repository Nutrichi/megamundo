Real Dimez is a rap duo made up of Bae-Luxe and Roxy. According to Rockstar,
the two have been friends since high school.

They turned money from shaking down local dealers into rap tracks and a
constant presence on social media. An early single with the local rapper
DWNPLY gave them their first big success.

Five years and a lot of trouble later, they are signed to Only Raw Records,
the label of Dre'Quan Priest and Boobie Ike. Rockstar writes that they hope
to score a second hit.
