Cal Hampton is a friend of Jason Duval and, like Jason, works with Brian
Heder. Rockstar describes him as someone who feels safest at home.

There he listens in on the radio traffic of the Coast Guard, with a few beers
and private browser tabs open. According to Rockstar, he has a taste for
conspiracy theories and is content at the bottom of society.

Rockstar writes that Cal is happy where he is, while Jason has bigger plans.
What role Cal plays in the story is not known.
