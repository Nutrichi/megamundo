In Grand Theft Auto VI, players can have one of their own cars delivered with
a phone app. The app is called Scooter Bros, the magazine Edge writes. For a
small fee, someone brings the car within seconds and leaves on a scooter.

IGN calls the app Scooter Brothers. The details come from the preview of
GTA VI in the November issue of Edge.

## A nod to GTA IV

The name refers to a well-known video from 2008, IGN writes. On the channel
FourPlayerPodcast on Justin.tv, now Twitch, Brad Simons rode a moped through
Liberty City in GTA IV. Simons kept calling another moped rider a "scooter
brother".

The video has more than 7.5 million views on YouTube. GTA V already nodded to
it: after switching to Trevor, players could sometimes see Trevor act out the
moment with a frightened passer-by.

## Weapons in the boot

The whole arsenal is stored in the boot of the car, according to Edge. Calling
up a car is therefore also the quick way to the weapons.

During a robbery, there is no time for a delivery. Players then leave a
getaway car around the corner, or take a passing car. That car may have a
tracker, and the driver may be armed, Edge writes.

Single-player GTA has never had a way to call up a car without cheats, IGN
notes. GTA Online has had one since its launch.

## Fuel and engine damage

Every vehicle has a fuel gauge and a display of engine health, according to
Edge. Cars can drive a long way before fuel runs low, and refuelling is a
short stop.

What a delivery costs is not known. Whether the riders on the scooter say
anything is not known either. How stealing cars with trackers works was
already in
