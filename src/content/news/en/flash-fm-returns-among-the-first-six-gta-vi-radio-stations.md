Rockstar Games has revealed six radio stations from Grand Theft Auto VI.
Flash FM returns from Vice City. Bad Bunny, Morgan Wallen and Lana Del Rey
voice some of the hosts, IGN reports. Previews are on the official site.

Rockstar announced the stations on 8 October on its Newswire and on a new
music page of the official website. It calls the selection the biggest and
widest-ranging of the series so far. The stations run from classic rock to
talk radio.

For each of the six stations, the music page plays a preview with three songs.
The page names the characters who host the stations, but not the people who
voice them. Those names come from IGN.

## The six stations

### Cocoteo FM

Modern Latin music, from bachata and merengue to dembow and reggaeton. The
hosts are El Martillo, voiced by Bad Bunny, and La Gata, voiced by RaiNao.

* Bad Bunny, EoO
* Axel Rulay ft. Farruko & El Alfa, Si Es Trucho Es Trucho (Remix)
* Romeo Santos & El Chaval de la Bachata, Canalla

### Back Country Radio

Renegade country for the Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn and beyond, with MW and
Delta Dawne. Morgan Wallen voices MW, Lana Del Rey voices Delta Dawne. Reports
come from Mud Dog Mike, a roving reporter and swamp meteorologist on his
airboat. He is also the not-so-secret lover of Delta, Rockstar writes. Jeremy
Dufrene voices him, according to IGN.

* David Allan Coe, Need a Little Time Off for Bad Behavior
* Loretta Lynn, Don't Come Home A-Drinkin' (With Lovin' On Your Mind)
* Hank Williams, Jr. & Waylon Jennings, The Conversation

### AfroBank FM

African music, from classics of the twentieth century and lesser-known gems
to amapiano and 3-step. The hosts are Burna, voiced by Burna Boy, and Palmsy,
voiced by Palms Trax.

* Asake, Joha
* Pat Thomas ft. Ebo Taylor, Gye Wani
* DJ Lag & Robot Boii ft. Kamo Mphela, Shay'sana

### The Chamber 106.6

Metal from the eighties and later, with DJ Kerry and DJ Tom. They are voiced
by Kerry King and Tom Araya of Slayer.

* Black Sabbath, I
* Pantera, I'm Broken
* Slayer, Raining Blood

### Flash FM

The pop station of Vice City returns, with Robyn and Alex. Robyn voices
herself, Alex is voiced by Alex Consani. Flash FM also played pop music in
Grand Theft Auto: Vice City in 2002.

* Charli xcx, Good Ones
* MGMT, Electric Feel
* Zara Larsson & PinkPantheress, Midnight Sun (Girls Trip)

### Dirty South Classics

Regional rap and hip hop classics from the South of the United States, with
Trick and Trina. Trick Daddy voices Trick, and Trina voices herself.

* David Banner ft. Lil' Flip, Like A Pimp
* JT Money ft. Solé, Who Dat
* DJ Khaled ft. Trick Daddy, Rick Ross & Pitbull, Born N Raised

## Four names were already circulating

Before the announcement, a list of fifteen station names circulated, partly
from leaked footage. Four of the six are on it: Back Country Radio, Cocoteo
FM, Dirty South Classics and The Chamber 106.6. Flash FM and AfroBank FM were
not. The other names on that list remain unconfirmed.

## More to come

Rockstar has not revealed the full list of stations, and how many there are
is not known. More information follows on the dynamic score of the game and
on new singles from Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, which holds 34 original
tracks.

GTA VI appears on 19 November for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S.
