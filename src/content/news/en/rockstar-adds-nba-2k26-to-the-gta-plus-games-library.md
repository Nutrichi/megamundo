Rockstar is putting NBA 2K26 in the GTA+ games library. Members on PlayStation
5 and Xbox Series X and S can play the full game from 10 March to 20 April.
The offer comes from a partnership with 2K Games.

Alongside the game come six types of skill boost for five matches and a
guaranteed Diamond Player Pack. An NBA 2K account is required for those.
Members also get 20 percent off buying the game outright.

## The car of the month

The Vapid Firebolt ASP is free to claim from The Vinewood Car Club through 1
April. Rockstar describes the off-roader as a flatbed that hauls loads by day
and races at night.

Members also get a basketball top and shorts and an orange camo tee added to
their wardrobe. Two chameleon paints are free at every Vehicle Workshop
through 1 April.

## The rest of the month

Salvage Yard income is doubled for owners. Community Series events pay up to
six times the usual reward this month. Work for Mr. Faber and QuickiePharm
deliveries pay double.

Membership keeps its standing benefits, among them a monthly deposit in the
Maze Bank account and the Vinewood Club app for managing businesses remotely.
