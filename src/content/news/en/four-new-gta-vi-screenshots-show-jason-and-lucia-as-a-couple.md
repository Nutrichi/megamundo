Four new screenshots of Grand Theft Auto VI show Jason and Lucia together.
They appear in issue 27 of LOVE magazine, which puts the pair on its cover.
The issue also carries an interview with the narrative team at Rockstar
Games.

LOVE magazine shared the images and a part of the article on Friday 2
October, RockstarINTEL reports. IGN Benelux reported on them on 4 October.
The four screenshots are not among the official downloads on the website of
Rockstar.

## A more modest American Dream

Rupert Humphries is senior vice president of narrative at Rockstar. He tells
LOVE that a version of today's world in a game has to be more over the top
than the real one. Otherwise players could just as well go outside, he says.
The aim is satire that is larger than life, not a mirror.

Lucia had to be someone who wants a better life and lets nothing stop her,
according to Humphries. The American Dream may be more modest now, he says.
It is a house for your family and your loans paid off, not wild success.
Miami and southern Florida are a front line for that, in his words.

## Two writers on Jason and Lucia

Writer Elizabeth Gauvey-Kern tells LOVE that both leads had to be
vulnerable, tough and flawed. They are right sometimes and wrong sometimes,
and they learn from each other. That made them authentic and a good team,
she says.

Writer Alexa D'Agostino adds that a fully realised person with their own
wants turns out perfectly imperfect. Such a character has clichés but is
still undeniably real, she says.

Rob Nelson, co-studio head of Rockstar North, is also interviewed in the
issue, RockstarINTEL writes. What he says in it is not yet known.

## The issue

Issue 27 costs 25 euro and reaches newsstands on 5 October, several gaming
sites report. The pre-orders for the GTA VI cover at the French distributor
KD Presse sold out within minutes, according to the same sites.

GTA VI comes out on 19 November.
