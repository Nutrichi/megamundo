Rockstar has released A Safehouse in the Hills in GTA Online. The update adds
mansion properties from Prix Luxury Real Estate in three locations. It also
brings a new mission strand and the Rockstar Mission Creator. Michael De Santa
appears as a neighbour.

The three sites are between Los Santos and Vinewood Hills, in the Tongva
Hills next to the Marlowe vineyard, and on a hillside overlooking the city.

## What is in a mansion

Each property has a garage for 20 vehicles, a helipad with a Volatus, a
redesigned wardrobe and a barber with a tattoo artist. Outdoors there is a
pool and a kennel, and a cat bed sits in the living room. Both animals can be
named.

Upgrades include a weapon workshop, an arcade and a vehicle workshop. Hired
security reduces raids on businesses. An AI assistant handles fast travel,
parties and a production boost of 24 hours.

## KnoWay Out

The new missions follow Avi Schwartzman against KnoWay, a self-driving car
start-up building a surveillance network. The strand is for one to four
players and does not require a mansion. It starts at the payphone in Little
Seoul.

The update also adds a Getaway Driver random event, four emotes and Missile
Lock-On Jammers for 50 more vehicles.
