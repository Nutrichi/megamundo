Rockstar has picked Old School Hits as the first featured Community Mission.
The job was built with the new Rockstar Mission Creator and pays out through
6 May. It is a tribute to three missions from the original Grand Theft Auto
trilogy.

The mission starts at a corona at Legion Square. Rockstar made it together
with a creator team and does not name the payout in plain text.

## What the Mission Series is

The Mission Series is a new way to play jobs made by the GTA Online Creator
community. It runs on PlayStation 5, on Xbox Series X and S, and on PC with
the Enhanced version.

Rockstar says the Race Series takes the spotlight at the next event in May.
More community-made jobs will be featured after that, but no titles or dates
are given.

## Bonuses this week

Madrazo Hits pay double for Bail Office owners, with extra for using
Madrazo's preferred weapon. Payphone Hits pay three times the usual amount
once a third Security Contract is done.

Open Wheel Races and the Time Trial also pay three times. The new Big
Minimus Energy livery is available at Vehicle Workshops and from the Luxury
Autos window display.
