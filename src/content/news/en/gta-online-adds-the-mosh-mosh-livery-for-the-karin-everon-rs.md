GTA Online has a new livery for the Karin Everon RS. It is called Mosh Mosh
and is available at every Vehicle Workshop. The Everon RS is an SUV. The
livery comes alongside a week of bonuses on Auto Shop work.

Auto Shop Client Jobs pay double, both in cash and in LS Car Meet
reputation. Delivering three vehicles completes the weekly challenge.

Madrazo Hits pay triple. Those require a Bail Office, and the work comes from
Martin Madrazo. Auto Shop Robbery Contract finales also pay double.

The Document Forgery Office is discounted this week, as are its upgrades and
modifications, and its sell missions pay a bonus.

## What is still unknown

Rockstar says three new Odd Jobs arrive in GTA Online next week but does not
say what they are. Whether the Mosh Mosh livery stays available after this
week is not stated either.
