GTA Online runs the Community Series Showcase from 12 March to 1 April. Jobs
made by players pay triple rewards for three weeks. Everyone who logs in
during that period receives GTA$1,000,000. Creators who have been featured
in the series receive GTA$10,000,000.

The series rotates every week. The first week mixes combat and racing, the
second is combat, and the third is racing.

## What players earn

Completing five Community Series jobs is worth up to GTA$1,500,000 on top of
the triple rewards. Rockstar says bonuses land in the in-game bank account
within 72 hours.

Weekly Challenges unlock a set of liveries and clothing. Winning three races
counts in the first week, three deathmatches in the second, and three races
again in the third.

A Vehicle Warehouse is free through 1 April. Claiming it requires being a
CEO with an Executive Office.

## Alongside the showcase

Rockstar is also placing St Patrick's Day collectibles across the map from
12 to 25 March. Players who find all 25 Lucky Clover balloons and the Golden
Clover receive a reward.

## What is still unknown

Which jobs are featured in the second and third week is not named in
advance. Whether the showcase returns is not stated.
