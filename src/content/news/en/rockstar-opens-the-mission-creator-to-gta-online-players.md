Rockstar has opened the Mission Creator to GTA Online players. The tool lets
anyone build missions across Los Santos and Blaine County and share them.
Missions can be made for up to eight players at a time.

Old School Hits was made by GTA Series Videos and is inspired by the original
Grand Theft Auto trilogy. It is the first entry in a new series of featured
player creations on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X and S, and PC in the Enhanced
version. The first featured creation, Old School Hits, goes live on 30 April.

## What the tool does

Creators can set objectives, control how NPCs behave, and use locations that
were previously off limits. Rockstar names the deck of the USS Luxington
aircraft carrier, the Cluckin' Bell factory and several banks.

The Creator is reached through the pause menu, under Online, Rockstar Creator,
Create a Mission. Five Rockstar-made missions are included as worked examples
and can be opened in the editor to see how they were built.

## Getting featured

Missions tagged for consideration can be picked up for a future Community
Series. Rockstar pays a one-time bonus of 10 million GTA$ to creators whose
mission is featured, along with a jacket for the in-game wardrobe.

Rockstar says more community missions will follow later in the year.
