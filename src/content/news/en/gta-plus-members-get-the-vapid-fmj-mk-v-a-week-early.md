GTA+ members in GTA Online can claim the new Vapid FMJ MK V supercar for
free. It is available at The Vinewood Car Club showroom a week before it goes
on public sale. Members also get a discount of one million on mansion
properties.

That discount applies to all three Prix Luxury Real Estate properties and
runs until 7 January 2026. Players who completed the New Listings missions
before 7 December get a larger reduction.

## What else members get

The Mansion Vehicle Workshop is upgraded free of charge. Business staff can
now be managed remotely through The Vinewood Club App, without going to the
premises.

Members also get early access to the Progen Luiva, a supercar without a roof
or windows. It goes on public sale on 18 December. Free Chameleon paints and
holiday costumes are included as well.

Freemode challenges and events pay four times the usual rate.

## What is still unknown

What the FMJ MK V costs once it goes on public sale is not stated. Whether
remote access to business staff is permanent or tied to the holiday period
is not mentioned either.
