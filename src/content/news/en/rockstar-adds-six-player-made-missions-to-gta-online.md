Rockstar has added six player-made missions to the Community Mission Series
in GTA Online. A seventh, Old School Hits, returns from an earlier round. The
missions run through 17 June. They are available on PlayStation 5, Xbox
Series X and S, and PC in the Enhanced version.

The six are Detective Story by BooCooSocial, Los Santos 2062 by ShelbyGR,
Juggernightmare by GTA Series Videos, Paleto Bay Slasher by Shadow-Break,
Running the Gauntlet by Sarah-Croft and The Scrumble Heist by SergeantDexter.

## What they are

Detective Story is a mid-century whodunit with classic cars and minigames.
Los Santos 2062 shows a futuristic version of the city. Juggernightmare is a
stealth mission in which players extract IAA agents without provoking the
Juggernauts.

Paleto Bay Slasher casts the player as an LSPD officer on a trail through
snow. Running the Gauntlet is built around the Epsilon Program. The Scrumble
Heist robs a Fleeca branch with decoy vehicles for the getaway.

## The bonus

Completing all seven pays a bonus once between 4 and 10 June, and again
between 11 and 17 June. Rockstar delivers it within 72 hours of completion.

The announcement does not say whether the missions stay playable after 17
June.
