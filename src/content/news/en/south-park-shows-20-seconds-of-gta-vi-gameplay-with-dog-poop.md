The latest episode of South Park shows almost 20 seconds of gameplay from
Grand Theft Auto VI. On a beach, a dog relieves itself and the walker picks up
the poop. Jason then attacks the walker with it, IGN writes.

The episode is called "Guinea Pig On A Stick". The main characters visit a
cattle ranch, where a character shows the game on a screen. That character is
voiced by Tish Hicks, according to the credits. Hicks also narrated the
gameplay trailer of GTA V.

The X account @GTASixInfo shared the full fragment in HD on 29 October.

## Known, but not seen before

Rockstar Games had already confirmed that players can pick up dog poop, IGN
writes. The fragment shows it in action for the first time. It also shows how
many passers-by fill the beach during play.

Whether Rockstar supplied the footage to South Park is not known. IGN calls it
genuine, exclusive gameplay.

## South Park and Rockstar

South Park has covered games before. The episode about World of Warcraft from
2006 is the best known. In 2018, Red Dead Redemption 2 played a large part in
two episodes.

Grand Theft Auto VI comes out on 19 November for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series
X and S.
