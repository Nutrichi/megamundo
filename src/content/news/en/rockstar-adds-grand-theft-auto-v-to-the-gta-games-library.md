Rockstar has added Grand Theft Auto V to the GTA+ Games Library. Members on
PC with the Enhanced version can now reach it as well. GTA+ members can also
claim a free Ocelot Stromberg through 13 July.

Players on all platforms can move their Story Mode and Online progress to
the latest versions, according to Rockstar. How long Grand Theft Auto V
stays in the library is not stated.

## The free car

The Ocelot Stromberg is a Sports Classic that works as a weaponised sports
car and as a submersible. Members can claim it at The Vinewood Car Club
showroom or through the Warstock Cache and Carry website.

The Teal and Purple Flip Chameleon paint and the matching wheel paint are
free at all Vehicle Workshops.

## Clothing and bonuses

The Feud Anniversary Baseball Shirt is added to member wardrobes at no cost.
Rockstar has also put Independence Day clothing in the wardrobe, including
the Green Emissive Lady Liberty Mask.

Smuggler Sell Missions pay double this week. Finding G's Cache pays five
times the usual amount, and the KnoWay Out mode pays double.
