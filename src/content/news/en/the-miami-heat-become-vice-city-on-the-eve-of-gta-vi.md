The Miami Heat play as Vice City on 18 November, the eve of the release of
Grand Theft Auto VI. The game against the Milwaukee Bucks gets its own court
and a new uniform. The club announced it on its website.

The Heat call the evening "A Night in Vice City". The club describes it as a
historic partnership and an event that has never been done before. Tip-off
at the Kaseya Center is at 7 PM local time. In Belgium and the Netherlands
that is 1 AM on 19 November, the day GTA VI comes out.

## The game against the Bucks

ESPN and local channel WPLG Local 10 show the game on television. On the
radio it runs on 560 WQAM and on the Spanish-language Radio Mambí 710 AM.
Tickets are sold through Ticketmaster. On the page of the Heat, the venue is
listed as Kaseya Center - Vice City.

<figure>

![The logos of ESPN, WPLG Local 10, 560 Sports WQAM and Radio Mambí WAQI 710 AM.](../../../assets/posts/gta-vi-heat-game-broadcast-channels.jpg)

<figcaption>Miami Heat, nba.com.</figcaption>

</figure>

The image at the top is a still from a drone video on the same page. It
shows the arena at night, with "Welcome to Vice City VI" on the roof and pink
light around the entrance. That sign was first switched on on 1 October.

## A court for one night

The Heat play on hardwood built specially for Vice City, for one night
only. The club so far only shows a blurred preview in pink light. What the
floor looks like is not yet known.

<figure>

![A blurred view of a basketball arena in pink light, with a bright court in the middle and the scoreboard above it.](../../../assets/posts/gta-vi-heat-activation-court.jpg)

<figcaption>Miami Heat, nba.com.</figcaption>

</figure>

Inside the Kaseya Center there will also be special activities, starting on
Opening Night, the first home game of the season. More follow on
18 November itself. The Heat promise more details later and do not yet say
what the activities are.

## A new uniform

For the night the Heat wear a new, completely reimagined uniform. It has not
been shown yet. The page only has the silhouette of a player against a pink
background, with a sign-up for the moment the uniform is revealed. The title
of the page calls it the 2026-27 Vice City Edition uniform.

<figure>

![The dark silhouette of a basketball player with his hands on his hips, against a pink background.](../../../assets/posts/gta-vi-heat-jersey-banner-silhouette.jpg)

<figcaption>Miami Heat, nba.com.</figcaption>

</figure>

The Heat have worn uniforms called Vice since the 2017-18 season. They are
inspired by the 1980s television series Miami Vice, with neon pink and
blue.

## The Vice City collection

A clothing line is already on sale. The Court Culture Vice City collection
is available in the Heat store at the Kaseya Center and online. Court
Culture is the clothing label of the Heat. The neck label of the shirts
carries the GTA VI logo next to that of the Heat.

<figure>

![Three people on a low wall between palm trees in Miami, wearing shirts with I love Vice City, Goodtime State and Vice City.](../../../assets/posts/gta-vi-heat-collection-banner.jpg)

Four pieces are on the page. Prices are in dollars:

* **Vice City Grill Oversized Tee**, 55 dollar, around 47 euro. A black shirt with a mouth full of gold teeth and the words Welcome to Vice City.
* **Vice City Souvenir Oversized Tee**, 55 dollar. A white shirt with "Went to Vice City and all I got was this lousy t-shirt!" on the back.
* **I 🩷 Vice City Crop**, 45 dollar, around 39 euro. A short dark shirt with a pink heart full of palm trees.
* **Vice City Pool Service Pocket Tee**, 55 dollar, made with the label Duvin. On the back an alligator in a swimming pool with a basketball hoop, the slogan "Leonida's Finest!" and the number 1-999-VICECITY.

## Part of a larger campaign

The night follows the "Welcome to Vice City" sign on the roof of the Kaseya
Center. Rockstar Games pays 975,000 dollar for it, around 840,000 euro, to
Miami-Dade County, which owns the arena. Whether Rockstar also pays for the
game night is not stated on the page.

The page of the Heat ends with a drone video of the skyline of Miami at
dusk.
