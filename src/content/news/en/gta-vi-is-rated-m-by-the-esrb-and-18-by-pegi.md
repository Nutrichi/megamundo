Grand Theft Auto VI has an official age rating. The American ESRB gives the
game an M for Mature. In Europe PEGI rates it 18. The ratings now appear on
the store pages of the game.

## What the ESRB lists

The ESRB names blood and gore, intense violence, mature humour, nudity,
strong language, strong sexual content and the use of drugs and alcohol, IGN
writes. So far the board has only published these short descriptors. A
longer summary, in which it often describes specific scenes, is not yet
available.

The rating also mentions in-game purchases. Earlier this year Rockstar Games
said there would be no monetisation in GTA VI, according to IGN. The news
site points out that the ESRB may also count the extra content of the
Ultimate Edition. Nothing official is known yet about an online mode for
GTA VI.

## Sex scenes in Australia and New Zealand

On the PlayStation Store in Australia and New Zealand, the rating of GTA VI
also lists sex scenes. The British and American store pages do not mention
them, IGN reports.

The short rating of GTA V on its store page only says sex, although that
game contains at least one sex scene. Whether the rating boards changed
their terms since then, or whether GTA VI has more scripted scenes, is not
known. The story of GTA VI centres on the relationship between Jason and
Lucia.

## Hot Coffee in San Andreas

In 2005 the ESRB changed the rating of GTA San Andreas to AO, Adults Only.
Modders had unlocked a hidden sex scene that was still in the game files.
Several shops then pulled the game for a while.

GTA VI is due on 19 November for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S.
