Camera crews are filming convoys of cars dressed as Grand Theft Auto VI vehicles in Miami. Fans think Rockstar Games is shooting a live-action trailer or video for the game. Rockstar has not said anything about it, IGN writes.

The cars carry brands from the game, such as Grotti and Progen Emerus, according to IGN. The same kind of convoy [drove through Miami last week](/news/real-cars-dressed-as-gta-vi-vehicles-drive-through-miami/) with Leonida plates and a police car in Vice City colours.

The fan account @GTAVI_Rodion posted a video of the filming on 20 October.

<blockquote class="twitter-tweet" data-dnt="true">
<p>Filming of a separate video or trailer for GTA VI continues in Miami🌴#GTAVI #GTA6</p>
<p><a href="https://twitter.com/GTAVI_Rodion/status/2112634591039361188">GTA 6 Raf (@GTAVI_Rodion) on X, 20 October 2026</a></p>
</blockquote>

## A trailer for the preload

According to the same account, Rockstar released the launch trailer early so that a second video, filmed in real life, can follow before the release. The ads, the Vice City graffiti and the sign on the stadium would then form the backdrop. The account thinks that video could appear on 12 November, when the preload starts. That is speculation; Rockstar has not announced a second video.

Earlier, on 15 October, Rockstar Games and the car customiser West Coast Customs showed up in Key Biscayne, according to a post by Uncle Ken.

What the footage will be used for is not known. The Miami Heat already play as Vice City on 18 November, in an evening the club calls [A Night in Vice City](/news/the-miami-heat-become-vice-city-on-the-eve-of-gta-vi/).
