The Grotti Itali Classic is on sale in GTA Online. The sports classic can be
bought at Legendary Motorsport. Rockstar is also starting its Lunar New Year
celebration, which runs to 4 March for part of the rewards.

Imani Tech Missile Lock-On Jammer support for the Itali Classic is fitted at
the vehicle workshop in the Agency, and in the mansion workshop for owners.

## Year of the Horse

The Senora Derby is a new stunt race for sports vehicles, set in the Grand
Senora Desert. It is part of the Lunar New Year Stunt Races for the next two
weeks.

Thirty-six Yuanbao are hidden around Los Santos. Each pays GTA$ and RP, and
finding all 36 pays an extra bonus. Horse tattoos and masks are free, and
logging in before 4 March gives a Year of the Horse tee.

## The rest of the week

Deadline Duet is a new mode for pairs. Both it and the original Deadline pay
triple rewards through 18 February.

The chance of finding the Pink Diamond in The Cayo Perico Heist is raised
until 18 February. The Gusenberg Sweeper is free from the Gun Van in the same
period.

## What is still unknown

Rockstar gives no price for the Itali Classic and no performance figures.
