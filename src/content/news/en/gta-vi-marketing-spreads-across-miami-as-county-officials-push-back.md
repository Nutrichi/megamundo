The marketing for Grand Theft Auto VI spreads further across Miami. The
stadium of the Miami Dolphins showed ads, and a Vice City blimp is on its way
south. Some county commissioners want to halt new deals with Rockstar Games,
The Miamian reports.

## Commissioners object

At a meeting last week, several commissioners of Miami-Dade County spoke out
against the campaign, the local news site The Miamian writes. The staff of
mayor Daniella Levine Cava is negotiating with Rockstar about Vice City
installations in public places.

Those talks include Miami International Airport, PortMiami and stations of
the Metromover. Rockstar would pay the county to temporarily dress them up as
Vice City.

Commissioner René Garcia said the whole plan felt really uncomfortable.
Garcia does not want the county to work with a game that depicts Miami as a
crime haven and a murder capital. Commissioner Danielle Cohen Higgins pointed
to a county rule against advertising that promotes violence.

## The price of the arena sign

The [Vice City sign on the Kaseya Center](/news/a-vice-city-sign-on-the-miami-heat-arena-costs-975000-dollar/)
brings the county 975,000 dollar. Commissioner Roberto Gonzalez suspects that
amount was chosen to stay below 1 million dollar. Above that limit, the
commission has to vote on a county contract.

Garcia says Rockstar spends at least 150 million dollar on marketing, and
finds the amount too low. The office of the mayor answers that the deal
covers a single rooftop display. Over seven months that comes to about
139,000 dollar a month, the office says.

## A vote on 20 October

A vote to let the mayor negotiate a deal above 1 million dollar almost turned
into a ban on all talks with Rockstar. Commissioner Oliver Gilbert postponed
that vote. The commission meets again on 20 October.

The city of Miami and Miami Beach already have their own deals, The Miamian
writes. Miami receives up to 1.2 million dollar for six temporary banners.
Miami Beach allows GTA VI signage on beach concession equipment, for up to
4 million dollar.

The next part of the campaign was expected from 15 October, IGN writes.
Miami journalist Joel Franco says an unnamed official doubts that the deals
will be ready in time.

## Stadium and blimp

Over the weekend, the Hard Rock Stadium of the Miami Dolphins carried ads for
GTA VI. The big screen showed the release date. After a touchdown, an
animation read "In for VI", a nod to the six points a touchdown scores, IGN
writes.

## What is not known

Rockstar has not responded to the objections of the commissioners. What
Rockstar pays for the stadium ads and the blimp is not known. Whether the
county deals go ahead depends on the meeting of 20 October.
