Rockstar Games has opened pre-orders for a collector's box for Grand Theft
Auto VI. The Vice City Collection holds eleven items around Macca the Gator,
a TV show in the game. It costs 399,99 euro and ships on 19 November.

Rockstar announced the box on 24 September on its Newswire. It is a limited
edition, sold only while supplies last. The full name is The Goodtime State,
and that is the nickname of Leonida, the state in which the game is set.

## Macca the Gator and his friends

Macca the Gator is a hit TV show in Leonida, Rockstar writes. The cartoon
alligator appears on most items in the box, usually with a drink in his hand.
Chunkee the Manatee, a flamingo, a crab and a sun in sunglasses are his
friends from the show.

All eleven items are shown on the
[Vice City Collection page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/vice-city-collection)
of the game. The descriptions below come from that page and from the product
page in the Rockstar Store.

## Eight collectibles

enjoys a smoke and a flamingo blood martini, Rockstar writes. The base twists
open and serves as a stash compartment.

## Price, size and shipping

The box costs 399,99 euro in the Rockstar Store, and free shipping does not
apply. It measures 48 by 34 by 32 centimetres. The store lists it as an adult
collectible for ages 18 and up. The game itself is not included.

How many boxes are made, Rockstar does not say. Pre-orders in Asia, Latin
America and Australia follow later, according to the Newswire. More clothing
and collectibles from the Grand Theft Auto VI Collection will also come to the
store soon.

Grand Theft Auto VI appears on 19 November for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X
and S.
