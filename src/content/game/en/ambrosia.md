Ambrosia lies inland, in the middle of the state, between the national park
to the north and the wetlands to the south. It is farmland: straight roads,
open fields and small settlements strung along them.

It is the least documented area on the map. Almost nothing of it has
appeared in the material Rockstar has released so far.

The community map marks large parts of Ambrosia with hatching. According to
its own legend, that means the area is speculative: the mappers have placed
something there without a confirmed source.
