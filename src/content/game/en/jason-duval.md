Jason Duval is one of the two main characters of Grand Theft Auto VI, next
to Lucia Caminos. Rockstar describes him as someone who wants an easy life,
while things only get harder.

According to Rockstar, he grew up among grifters and criminals. He joined the
Army to put a troubled youth behind him. After that he ended up in the
Leonida Keys, working for local drug runners.

He lives rent-free in a property owned by Brian Heder. In return he helps
with local shakedowns, Rockstar writes in its description of Brian.

Rockstar suggests that Jason may be ready for something else. Meeting Lucia
could be the best or the worst thing that has happened to him. How that turns
out is not known.
