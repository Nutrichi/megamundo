Rockstar has added new items to its own web store. The collection holds
clothing, posters, stickers and two scented candles. A crewneck sweatshirt
with a varsity-style logo is the main piece. Prices are not in the
announcement.

There is also a set of tees printed with images from older Rockstar titles.
Rockstar names Grand Theft Auto: Vice City, Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas
and Midnight Club: Los Angeles.

## An in-game version as well

Players who complete all three New Listings missions in GTA Online before
7 December receive the in-game version of the black varsity sweatshirt. It
is added to the wardrobe as part of the Gold Tier rewards.

That is the only part of the announcement that costs nothing.

## Candles, posters and deadlines

The two scented candles were made with fragrance designers Joya Studio.
Rockstar has also added five poster sets and a sticker collection with
reflective logo designs.

Rockstar gives three deadlines for orders to arrive before 24 December. In
the United Kingdom that is 19 December, in Europe 18 December. For North
America and the rest of the world it is 12 December.

The announcement does not say how long the items stay available. One item is
described as included while supplies last.
