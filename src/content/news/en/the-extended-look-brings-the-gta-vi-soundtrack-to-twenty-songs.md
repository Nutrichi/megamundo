Twenty songs from GTA VI are known by name. They come from the three trailers
and from the Extended Look of 27 August, GameSpot writes. Fifteen of them
appear in that 25 minute video. Rockstar Games has published no full
soundtrack.

## The twenty confirmed songs

GameSpot lists per track where it first appeared, with the timecode in the
Extended Look.

* Tom Petty, Love Is a Long Road (trailer 1)
* The Pointer Sisters, Hot Together (trailer 2)
* Wang Chung, Everybody Have Fun Tonight (trailer 2)
* Zenglen, Child Support (trailer 2)
* Tammy Wynette, Talkin' to Myself Again (trailer 2)
* Birdman featuring Lil Wayne, Pop Bottles (0:08)
* Sexyy Red and Tay Keith, Pound Town (0:50)
* Skrilla, Kodak Black (1:56)
* The Bellamy Brothers, Let Your Love Flow (6:00)
* Def Leppard, Love Bites (6:43)
* Depeche Mode, People Are People (8:21)
* Captain and Tennille, But I Think It's a Dream (9:50)
* Prefab Sprout, Cars and Girls (10:42)
* !!! featuring Meah Pace, Off the Grid (12:25)
* DRÆVEN, GTA VI Theme Song (13:41)
* Cliff Richard, Devil Woman (16:49)
* Chimbala and Omega, Se Me Nota (Agarrame) (20:10)
* Phil Collins, Against All Odds (21:37)
* Aluna and Jayda G, Mine O' Mine (22:03)
* Bob Moses and Elderbrook, Inner Light (25:44)

The range runs from eighties pop and country to modern hip hop and dance.
Whether these tracks also appear in the game itself, and on which station, is
not known.

## Artists who have hinted at a role

A number of musicians have suggested they are involved, without Rockstar
confirming it. Travis Scott posted a GTA VI styled teaser in September 2026 and
tagged Rockstar. In July 2025 a car with the licence plate "GTA6 VI6" appeared
in his video for 2000 Excursion.

ScHoolboy Q replied to a fan on Twitter in February 2024 that he would be on
the radio in GTA VI. T-Pain said in a livestream in January 2024 that he works
with Rockstar on the game. According to him he had to stop role playing on the
server NoPixel because of a conflict of interest.

Ski Mask the Slump God wrote on Instagram that tracks by him and by the late
XXXTentacion are in the game. Which songs those are, he did not say. The band
Panama said in a since deleted Instagram comment that Back to Life, made with
Poolside, would be in GTA VI.

Rapper Skrilla said publicly that Doot Doot would appear in the game. Neon
Indian hinted in a podcast that his link with Rockstar has not ended. Freddie
Gibbs is named in an interview with PC Gamer by content creator HipHopGamer,
who says a rapper features in a side mission.

Megan Thee Stallion posted a video in March 2025 with a palm tree backdrop that
resembled the promotional artwork. She later posted the same video as an
advertisement for her own tequila brand.

## Songs from the CyberLeek footage

Alongside a document with 25 songs, tracks were also heard in the footage from
the CyberLeek hack, GameSpot writes with reference to Kotaku. Among them are
Kansas, Stevie Nicks, Jamiroquai, Joan Jett and the Blackhearts, Tate McRae,
Glen Campbell and George Strait. The material itself is unverified and Rockstar
has not commented on it.

One detail does point in that direction. Rockstar added the Stevie Nicks track
to its official GTA VI playlist on Spotify.

## Fifteen radio stations, none confirmed

A list of fifteen stations is circulating, part of it from the leaked footage.
Rockstar has confirmed no station. Three names return from earlier games:
Emotion 98.3 and V Rock from Vice City, and WorldWide FM from GTA V.

* Back Country Radio, country
* Circoloco Records Radio, electronic dance
* Cocoteo FM, reggaeton and Latin
* Dirty South Classics, hip hop from the nineties and the noughties
* Emotion 98.3, contemporary
* Honey FM, R&B and soul
* Kaleidoscope FM, disco
* La Ola, Latin
* Radio On.U, reggae
* Stockyard FM, pop
* Symphony FM, classical
* The Chamber 106.6, metal
* The Wipe, genre unknown
* V Rock, rock
* WorldWide FM, world music

GTA VI appears on 19 November for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S.
