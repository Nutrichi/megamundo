GTA Online has a new adversary mode called Deadline Duet. Two players ride
Nagasaki Shotaro bikes and use their light trails to destroy opponents. The
mode pays triple rewards for two weeks. It is launched from the Featured
Series.

Winning two matches satisfies the Weekly Challenge. Whether Deadline Duet
stays in the game after those two weeks is not stated.

## Valentine's week

Working as an Associate or Bodyguard pays four times the usual GTA$ this
week. The Nagasaki Shotaro itself is half price at Legendary Motorsport.

The Pfister Astrale is new this week, a two-door Sports Classic that Rockstar
describes as a throwback to the 1990s. GTA+ members get early access to it.
No price is given.

## Free items

Logging in to GTA Online through 18 February gets the Valentines Onesie.
Nightclubs serve free champagne this week and next.

Valentine's clothing returns at half price. Short Trips pay double, and Meth
Lab properties and upgrades are discounted for MC leaders.
